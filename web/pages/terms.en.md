## What REON is

REON is a fan-run revival of the **Mobile Game Boy Adapter** service — the
Mobile System GB — which Nintendo and Konami operated in Japan and shut down
in 2002. It exists so that the online features built into those Game Boy
games can work again.

REON is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to** Nintendo, Game
Freak, The Pokémon Company, Konami, or any other rights holder. All
trademarks belong to their owners. The service is run by hobbyists, free of
charge, and nothing here is sold.

REON does not supply games. You bring your own copy of a game you already
own.

## Your account

You need an account to use the service. When you create one:

- Give a working e-mail address. It is how your account is recovered.
- Keep your password to yourself. Anything done through your account is
  treated as done by you.
- One account per person. [Decision: whether more than one is allowed.]

Your account carries a second, separate password that the **game** uses to
log in — the one written into your `mobile_config.bin`. It is not your
website password, and it can be rolled at any time from
[Your Account](/user/summary.php). How it is stored, and why, is explained in
the [Privacy Policy](/privacy.php).

## What you may not do

- Attack, overload, or try to break into the service or other players'
  accounts.
- Use REON to harass anyone, or to send messages you would not want attached
  to your name.
- Distribute copyrighted material through the mail service.
- Run automated clients that place load on the service beyond what a game
  playing normally would.

We may suspend or remove an account that does these things. [Decision:
whether there is an appeal, and to whom.]

## What other players can see

Some parts of REON are public **by design**, because they were public on the
original service: the ranking pages, the Trade Corner, and the Battle Tower
show the name your game uploaded, your sub-region, and any message the game
let you write — to anyone, without signing in.

Treat anything your game uploads as public. Do not put a real name, an
address, or anything private into an in-game name or message.

## Mail

REON carries mail between players, and can send to and receive from real
internet addresses. It is a game service, not a mail provider:

- Deleted mail sits in the trash for 30 days and is then gone for good.
- Messages can be lost. Do not use REON for anything that matters.
- Mail that breaks the rules above may be blocked.

## The service may stop

REON is run in someone's spare time on a single server. It can be slow, it
can be down, it can lose data, and it can stop existing. It is offered **as
is**, with no warranty and no promise that it will be there tomorrow. Keep
your own backups of anything you care about, including your save files.
