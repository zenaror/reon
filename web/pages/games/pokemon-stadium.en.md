## Get started

**Pokémon Stadium 2** for the Nintendo 64 reads a Pokémon Crystal cartridge
through the **Transfer Pak**, and the Japanese release carries a
**Mobile Stadium** mode built for this service.

did. Set the adapter up on the <a href="/guide.php">Get started</a> page and
play Crystal first; this tab is what you can do afterwards.</div>
